Our Story

Everyday tools
and video games

TIE-Games is an independent studio based in Dresden, Germany, making software and games. Our apps take a chore off your hands and get out of your way - from turning camera footage into finished videos to playing your favourite games in a new way, together. Our video games teach you through play, and one more try always feels within reach.

Whatever we make respects the people using it. No tutorials that won't end, no notifications begging you to come back, no accounts you don't need and no data harvesting under the guise of personalization. Your files and your time stay yours.
