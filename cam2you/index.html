Cam2You

From your GoPro to your YouTube channel, without the busywork.

Cam2You gets the videos off your GoPro, lets you cut and join them, and uploads them to your YouTube channel — as one video or as a playlist. It is made by TIE-Games, an independent studio in Dresden, Germany.

What it does

  • Finds your videos on a GoPro connected by USB, over the camera's Wi-Fi or on your home network, as well as on memory cards, in folders and in your phone's gallery.
  • Previews and cuts: trim the start and end of each video, or keep several parts of it, frame-exact.
  • Joins videos into one with a transition of your choice, or uploads them as a playlist in the order you arrange.
  • Suggests names from where and when the videos were recorded, such as Rovinj, Croatia (August 2026), using the camera's GPS and a place database built into the app.
  • Uploads reliably: a queue that resumes after interruptions and waits for YouTube's daily limits.

Cam2You runs on Windows and Android; an iPhone version is planned.

How Cam2You uses your Google account

To upload, you connect your YouTube channel with Google sign-in. Cam2You asks for access to your YouTube account (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube) and uses it only to:

  • upload the videos you choose, with the title, description and privacy you set;
  • create playlists and add your uploaded videos to them;
  • check whether YouTube has processed each upload, and whether it is public or private;
  • delete a video from YouTube only when you ask for it and confirm it.

Your videos travel directly from your device to YouTube. TIE-Games runs no servers that receive your videos or your Google data, and Cam2You contains no analytics or advertising. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
